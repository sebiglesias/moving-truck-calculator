Frequently Asked Questions

What size moving truck do I need for a 2-bedroom apartment?

A 15 ft truck (like a U-Haul 15' Super Mover) can handle a standard 2-bedroom apartment if packed tightly. If you have a garage, large sectional sofa, or patio furniture, step up to a 20 ft truck to avoid leaving items behind on long-distance moves.

Why should I never use large boxes for books?

Books, tools, and liquids are dense. A large box full of books easily exceeds 80–100 lbs, causing the cardboard bottom to buckle and risking severe lower back injury. Keep all heavy dense items in Small 1.5 cu. ft. boxes.

What size truck can you drive with a regular US driver's license?

Any rental truck up to 26,000 lbs Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR)—which includes 10 ft, 15 ft, 20 ft, and 26 ft U-Haul, Budget, and Penske trucks—can be legally driven with a standard Class D non-commercial driver's license in all 50 US states.
